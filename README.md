Yahan campus ki poori jankari milti hai — facilities, teachers, managers, events aur bahut kuch!

---

## 🌟 Project Ka Uddeshya (Purpose)

Jab ek **naya student Sunstone join karna chahta hai**, toh uske mann mein bahut sawal hote hain:

- 🤔 *"Bhopal campus kaisa hai?"*
- 🤔 *"Yahan kaunsi facilities milti hain?"*
- 🤔 *"Teachers aur management kaisa hai?"*
- 🤔 *"Kya events hote hain campus mein?"*
- 🤔 *"Orientation kaise hoti hai?"*

**Yeh portal in sabhi sawaalon ka jawab deta hai — ek hi jagah par!**

---

## 👥 Yeh Portal Kiske Liye Hai?

| 👤 User | 📋 Unke Liye Kya Hai |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **New / Prospective Students** | Campus choose karne se pehle poori jankari lena |
| 📚 **Current Students** | Orientation, events, attendance, e-learning access |
| 👨‍🏫 **Teachers & Faculty** | Campus activities aur events ki information |
| 🏢 **Campus Head & Manager** | Campus showcase aur student onboarding support |

---

## ✨ Portal Ki Khaasiyatein (Features)

### 🏠 Homepage
Campus ka pehla impression — navigation aur overview sab ek jagah.

### 📋 Orientation Details
- Naye students ke liye joining process
- Step-by-step orientation schedule
- Campus rules aur guidelines

### 🏆 Campus Facilities & Benefits
- Hostel, Cafeteria, Library, Labs
- Placement support
- Industry connect programs
- Jo bhi suvidhaen Bhopal campus deta hai

### 👨‍🏫 Faculty & Management Info
- Campus ke teachers ki jankari
- Head aur Manager ka parichay
- Contact details aur departments

### 🎉 Campus Events
- College-level events aur activities
- Cultural Fest details
- Ideathon / Hackathon information

### 🏅 Achievements
- Students aur batch ki achievements
- Awards aur recognitions
- Campus ki success stories

### 🖼️ Gallery
- Campus ki real photos
- Student life ki jhanki
- Events aur celebrations ke snapshots

### 📚 E-Learning
- Online resources aur study material
- E-learning project links

### 📊 Attendance
- Student attendance tracking page

## 🚀 Kaise Use Karein? (How to Run)

Koi installation nahi chahiye! Bas yeh 3 steps follow karein:

```
1. Is repository ko download ya clone karein
2. index.html file ko browser mein open karein
3. Menu se kisi bhi section mein jaaein
```

> 🌐 **Supported Browsers:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari
> 📶 **Internet:** Zaroorat nahi — offline bhi kaam karta hai!
